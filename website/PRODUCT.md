# Knowledge Graph Exploration Survey

An English academic companion to the systematic review of interaction, guidance, visualisation, backends, scalability, and evaluation in knowledge graph exploration.

Readers are researchers and practitioners who need to find relevant reports, inspect the primary evidence, and compare approaches under their reported conditions. The catalogue contains 242 included primary reports. The separate retrieval register records 991 distinct reports sought; report, system, study, participant, annotation, query, and deployment counts retain separate units.

The core actions are searching and filtering the catalogue, reading source-linked report details, comparing two to four reports, interpreting evaluation designs and samples, following named system families, reading the methods and PRISMA accounting, and downloading bibliographic and structured data.

Publication and source statements use the review’s recorded evidence. Implemented or demonstrated behaviour, proposed methods, future work, and missing evidence remain distinct. A landing-page match establishes only its scoped identity. Sources unavailable for retrieval retain undetermined eligibility. Companion reports do not imply independent cohorts. Source PDFs remain at their publishers or repositories.

The literature cutoff is 29 August 2026; the verification date is 6 October 2026. Search-provenance and retrieval limitations remain visible. The future KG exploration application is described in a separate requirements document, with RDF/SPARQL and property-graph/Cypher support; this companion provides the review catalogue and reading tools.
